Process Transparency

Our 6-Step Export Execution Workflow

A disciplined, step-by-step export protocol that mitigates commercial risk, protects cargo quality, and ensures timely delivery to international discharge ports.

01
Specification & Samples

Buyer provides technical grade, moisture, mesh, or dimensional requirements. Pre-shipment lab samples dispatched by courier.

02
Proforma & Contract

Formal Proforma Invoice issued specifying Incoterm (FOB/CIF), packaging standards, payment terms, and delivery schedules.

03
Batch Sourcing & QC

Procurement from vetted suppliers. Rigorous batch testing for moisture, purity, microbiological, or dimensional tolerances.

04
Packing & Stuffing

ISPM-15 palletization, moisture desiccants, and container stuffing supervised at ICD or port CFS with photo reporting.

05
Customs & Sealing

Export customs clearance, Shipping Bill generation, plant quarantine inspection, and tamper-evident container sealing.

06
Transit & BL Release

Ocean vessel departure, container tracking updates, and prompt dispatch of Clean Bill of Lading and trade documents.

Quality Assurance

Quality Control & Regulatory Compliance

We adhere strictly to verified quality assurance standards. No unverified certifications or unsubstantiated claims are published.

01

Raw Material Intake & Origin Verification

Every consignment starts with origin tracking. For agricultural produce, grain length, moisture, and purity are checked upon arrival at processing mills. For engineering goods, raw billets and brass ingots are verified via Mill Test Certificates (EN 10204 3.1).

Visual & Moisture Check Raw Material MTC Review Origin Batch Lot Numbering
02

In-Process Processing & Sorting

Commodities undergo mechanical cleaning, de-stoning, grading, and electrostatic sortex color sorting to remove foreign matter, broken grains, and discolored pieces.

Auto-Sortex 99.98% Purity Magnet Separation Screening & Sizing
03

Pre-Shipment Laboratory Analysis

Accredited laboratory testing is carried out per lot. Tests include chemical assays (Curcumin %, Piperine %, Oil content), heavy metal detection, pesticide residue limits (EU/US FDA compliant), and microbiological counts (Salmonella, E. Coli, Aflatoxins).

Certificate of Analysis (COA) Pesticide Residue Free Non-GMO Declaration
04

Independent Third-Party Inspection

Upon buyer instruction, independent international inspection agencies (such as SGS, Bureau Veritas, or Intertek) are invited to draw representative samples, witness loading, check weights, and seal containers.

SGS / BV Facilitation Weight & Quality Certification Container Sealing Witness
05

Container Stuffing & ISPM-15 Fumigation

Stuffing is conducted in clean, dry, odor-free containers. Solid wood packing material conforms strictly to ISPM-15 standards with accredited heat treatment and fumigation stamps. Silica gel desiccants are suspended to prevent container rain.

ISPM-15 Heat Treated Pallets Fumigation Certificate Moisture Desiccant Bags Tamper-Evident High-Security Bolt Seal
